About

Editing that makes the story feel effortless.

I’m Fiza Azad, a video editor and digital creative focused on short-form storytelling for social media. My work moves between quick, energetic explainers and quieter documentary pieces rooted in people, culture and place.

I care about what the viewer feels from one cut to the next: where attention begins, when a moment needs space, and how sound, type and pacing can make an idea land.

My background in communication design and my current work in user acquisition at Jobifer help me approach every edit with both visual judgment and an understanding of audiences.

  • Short-form editing
  • Story and pacing
  • Text and captions
  • Sound and music
  • Social media content
